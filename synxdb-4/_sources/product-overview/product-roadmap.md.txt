# Product Release Roadmap

This document outlines the planned development and release schedule for our products.

## 2024

**Apache Cloudberry™ (Incubating) incubation:** Cloudberry was officially approved as an Apache Incubator project.

:::{note}
Apache Cloudberry™ (Incubating) is an advanced and mature open-source Massively Parallel Processing (MPP) database, derived from the open-source version of the Greenplum Database® but built on a more modern PostgreSQL kernel and with more advanced enterprise capabilities. Cloudberry can serve as a data warehouse and can also be used for large-scale analytics and AI/ML workloads.

:::

:::{important}
**Apache Disclaimer**

Apache Cloudberry is an effort undergoing incubation at The Apache Software Foundation (ASF), sponsored by the Apache Incubator. Incubation is required of all newly accepted projects until a further review indicates that the infrastructure, communications, and decision making process have stabilized in a manner consistent with other successful ASF projects. While incubation status is not necessarily a reflection of the completeness or stability of the code, it does indicate that the project has yet to be fully endorsed by the ASF.
:::

## 2025

### Q1: Existing Greenplum replacement

- **SynxDB v1:** Based on Greenplum 5.x and Postgres 8.3.
- **SynxDB v2:** Based on Greenplum 6.x and Postgres 9.4.
